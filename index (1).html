How v3 tracking works

Whole-pitch tracking. The app now follows the ball across the entire strip between the dashed lines and fits a speed line through every sighting. You'll see lime dots trail behind the ball — that's the app locking on.

Lines A and B are calibration. Drag them onto the two creases and enter the real distance between those creases. They no longer trigger anything; they just teach the app how many meters one screen-width is.

People are ignored automatically. Anything moving slower than ~25 km/h — walkers, the bowler's run-up — fails the speed fit and is discarded.

If balls are missed: raise sensitivity until rolling a ball across the frame leaves a visible dot trail. More light and a bright ball still help the most on budget cameras.
