How single-pole mode works

The lime pole. Drag it anywhere along the pitch — mid-pitch is ideal. The app tracks the ball's flight and reports its speed the moment the ball crosses this line.

The blue ruler calibrates everything. Drag its two round handles so the ruler exactly covers the stumps (standard stumps are 0.71 m — already filled in). If you'd rather use something else — a marked pole, a chair — stretch the ruler over it and type its real height. This one ruler teaches the app how big a meter looks on screen.

No speed limit. Slow rollers, spin, even a gentle lob — all measured. People are filtered by shape, not speed: a ball is a small compact blob of motion, a person is a tall column. If a walker occasionally sneaks a reading in, just disarm between overs.

Lime dots trail whatever is being tracked. Roll a ball across the frame — if dots follow it, you're calibrated. If not, raise sensitivity.
