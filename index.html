Setup guide

1. Position. Tripod or stable ledge, side-on to the pitch, roughly at the halfway point. Both creases must be visible in frame.

2. Gates. Drag the left line onto the bowling crease and the right line onto the batting crease. Enter the real distance between those two spots in meters — measure it once with a tape or pace it out.

3. Arm. Tap "Arm gun". When the ball crosses the first line a timer starts; when it crosses the second, the speed flashes on screen. The gun re-arms itself after each delivery.

False triggers? Lower the sensitivity, or angle the camera so people and nets stay out of the horizontal band between the two dashed lines.

Accuracy. This measures average speed across the gates. Release speed (what broadcasters show) is a bit higher because the ball slows in flight.
