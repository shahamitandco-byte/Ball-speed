Setup & tuning guide

Meters. The A and B bars show live motion at each gate. Wave a hand across a gate — its bar should spike past the middle marker and glow lime. If a real ball doesn't make it glow, raise sensitivity. If it glows from people or wind in the nets, lower sensitivity or move the gates to cleaner background.

Rapid deliveries. The gun re-arms itself about a second after each reading. If a ball trips only one gate, it self-clears in 2.5 s — no need to touch anything between balls.

Light matters. A low-cost camera in dim light = motion blur and missed balls. Bright daylight or strong floodlights, a white or orange ball, and a plain background behind the pitch line improve detection more than any setting.

Framing. Keep both gates in the middle 80% of the frame and the pitch roughly horizontal. Closer camera = bigger ball = easier detection, as long as both creases stay in view.
