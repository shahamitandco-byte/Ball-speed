What v7 changed + field tuning

People are skipped, not blocking. Earlier versions grabbed the biggest moving thing (usually the bowler) and gave up. v7 finds every moving object, throws away the person-shaped ones, and tracks the compact ball-shaped ones — even while the bowler, batsman, and keeper all move.

Physics lock-on. Once a ball track starts, each new sighting must be where the ball is predicted to be. Random motion elsewhere can't hijack a track mid-flight.

Dot test still rules. Roll a ball while people walk around — dots should follow the ball, not the people. Tune sensitivity until they do.

Status line is the debugger. "tracking: N pts" should climb during a delivery. If it climbs but no speed comes, note the highest N and report it.
